Privacy Policy

Updated: 7 October 2026

Who we are

Abiryva Technology Limited provides technology consulting services. Contact us about privacy at info@abiryva.com.

Information you provide

When you contact us, we receive the details you choose to share. The website enquiry form, when available, collects your name, email address, organisation, optional phone number, service interest, message and agreement to be contacted about that enquiry. Do not submit passwords, confidential documents or sensitive personal information.

How information is used

We use enquiry information to respond, understand your requirements and discuss potential services. Agreeing to contact about an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

Service providers

Vercel hosts the website and enquiry endpoint. When email delivery is configured, Resend processes the enquiry to send it to our team. The optional booking calendar is operated by Calendly; booking details are processed through that service. These providers may process technical information and data outside your country. Google Fonts supplies font files on pages that request them and receives connection information such as your IP address.

Technical information and storage

Hosting and security systems may process information such as IP addresses, request times and device or browser information. The enquiry endpoint does not log message contents or contact details. The site remembers your privacy choice locally on your device. See the Cookie Policy.

Retention and security

Enquiries are delivered to our business mailbox and retained only as needed to respond, manage the relationship and meet applicable obligations. We use technical and organisational safeguards to protect information. Please contact us to discuss retention or request deletion of your enquiry.

Your requests

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, to object to processing, or to withdraw agreement to further contact. Requests are handled subject to applicable requirements and any information we need to retain. You may also raise concerns with the relevant data-protection authority.

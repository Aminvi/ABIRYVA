1. Introduction

Welcome to Abiryva Technology Limited ("Abiryva", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store and safeguard your information when you visit our website, contact us, or engage our technology consulting services.

By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information.

Information You Provide

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Company or organisation
  • Job title (where applicable)
  • Information submitted through our contact forms
  • Information contained in emails or other communications you send us

Information Collected Automatically

When you browse our website, our servers may automatically collect limited technical information including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of access
  • Pages viewed
  • Referring website (where available)

This information helps us maintain website security, improve performance and diagnose technical issues.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests.
  • Provide consulting and professional services.
  • Communicate with prospective and existing clients.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Protect our systems against fraud, abuse and cyber threats.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, we process personal information under one or more of the following lawful bases:

  • Your consent.
  • Performance of a contract.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Our legitimate business interests.

5. Cookies

Our website uses only cookies and technologies necessary for the secure operation and proper functioning of the website.

We do not currently use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity or other third-party analytics platforms to track visitors.

For more information please read our Cookie Policy.

6. Sharing Your Information

We may disclose information only where necessary to:

  • Trusted service providers supporting our operations.
  • Professional advisers.
  • Regulatory authorities where legally required.
  • Law enforcement where required by law.

Any third parties engaged by us are expected to implement appropriate security measures to protect personal information.

7. International Data Transfers

As a technology consulting firm serving organisations across Africa and internationally, your information may be processed in countries outside your country of residence.

Where applicable, we implement reasonable safeguards designed to protect personal information during international transfers.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate administrative, technical and organisational security measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

While we strive to use commercially reasonable safeguards, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide requested services.
  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Enforce contractual agreements.

When information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

10. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Request a copy of your personal information.

11. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy or security practices of those websites and encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website and professional services are intended for businesses, government organisations and professionals.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements or business practices.

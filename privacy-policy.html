Privacy Policy

Updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

Abiryva provides technology consulting services. Contact us about privacy at info@abiryva.com.

Information you provide

When you contact us, we receive the details you choose to share. The website enquiry form, when available, collects your name, email address, organisation, optional phone number, service interest, message and agreement to be contacted about that enquiry. Do not submit passwords, confidential documents or sensitive personal information.

How information is used

We use enquiry information to respond, understand your requirements and discuss potential services. Agreeing to contact about an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

Service providers

Vercel hosts the website and enquiry endpoint. When email delivery is configured, Resend processes the enquiry to send it to our team. The optional booking calendar is operated by Calendly; booking details are processed through that service. These providers may process technical information and data outside your country. Google Fonts supplies font files on pages that request them and receives connection information such as your IP address.

Technical information and storage

Hosting and security systems may process information such as IP addresses, request times and device or browser information. Our application code does not intentionally write message contents or contact details to application logs. Hosting and email providers may retain request metadata and email content under their own policies and configured settings. The site remembers your privacy choice locally on your device. See the Cookie Policy.

Retention and security

Enquiries are delivered to our business mailbox and retained only as needed to respond, manage the relationship and meet applicable obligations. We use technical and organisational safeguards to protect information. Please contact us to discuss retention or request deletion of your enquiry.

Your requests

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, to object to processing, or to withdraw agreement to further contact. Requests are handled subject to applicable requirements and any information we need to retain. You may also raise concerns with the relevant data-protection authority.

Website traffic statistics

Optional first-party analytics produces daily aggregate counts of public-page views, discovery-call link clicks and successfully sent enquiries. It groups page views by public page, broad referral source, country and device category, with a maximum retention of 90 days. Collection starts only after your analytics choice; you can withdraw it through Cookie settings. Browser privacy signals disable collection. We do not store visitor identities, contact details, raw IP addresses, full referrer URLs or persistent analytics identifiers in these totals. A keyed, short-lived rate-limit counter helps limit abuse. Portal pages are excluded and traffic reports are restricted to authorised Abiryva staff. Statistics are indicative: repeat views count separately, some bots may be included and discovery-call clicks do not confirm a booking. Provider logs and backups are governed separately as described above.

Booking email verification

Before opening the booking calendar on this website, we send a verification code to your email through Resend. Verification records in Upstash Redis contain your email address and a protected code value and expire after ten minutes. Protected email and network identifiers are used for request limits for up to one hour. After verification, your email is passed to Calendly to prefill the booking form. These providers process information to deliver verification and scheduling services.

Client and staff portals

Clerk handles account sign-in. Upstash Redis stores workspace assignments, project updates, shared documents and support requests. Client accounts can access only their assigned workspace; authorised Abiryva staff manage delivery and access.

Shared document copies and support requests expire after 90 days. Workspace records expire 180 days after the latest staff update; access assignments expire 180 days after being granted or renewed. Staff audit records identify the account, action and workspace and expire after 90 days. Audit records do not contain document contents, support messages or credentials. Provider backups and logs follow their own retention settings.

Resend publishes a standard 30-day retention period for email and log data on Free, Pro and Scale plans. Provider settings and our business mailbox may retain separate copies. Contact info@abiryva.com for questions about retention, deletion or the contracting entity for your engagement.

This Cookie Policy explains how Abiryva Technology Limited ("we", "our", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly and improve user experience.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure the website operates correctly
  • Enhance user experience
  • Analyze website traffic and performance
  • Remember user preferences

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required for basic functionality such as page navigation and access to secure areas.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve it.

Functional Cookies

Allow the website to remember choices you make for a more personalized experience.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use trusted third-party services (such as analytics providers) that place cookies to collect usage data. These third parties have their own privacy policies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect how the website functions.

6. Legal Basis (GDPR & NDPR)

Where required by law, we rely on your consent to use non-essential cookies. You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

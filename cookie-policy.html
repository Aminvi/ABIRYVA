Cookie Policy

Updated: 7 October 2026

How this website remembers your choices

This website stores your privacy choice in your browser’s local storage under abiryvaPrivacyChoiceV1. This preference remembers whether you allow optional booking content. It does not contain your name or contact details.

Optional Calendly content

The embedded booking calendar is provided by Calendly. It is not loaded automatically. Clicking “Load booking calendar” loads third-party content for that visit, which may use cookies or similar technologies and receives technical information such as your IP address. You can instead use the direct booking link, call us or email us.

See Calendly’s privacy notice for its processing practices.

Analytics and marketing

The site does not include a configured analytics or marketing tracking service. Hosting systems may process request and security logs. If additional tracking is introduced, this notice and the privacy controls must be updated.

Change your choice

Use “Cookie settings” in any page footer. Selecting “Essential only” removes an embedded calendar from the current page. You can clear the stored preference through your browser settings. Third-party cookies already stored by Calendly are managed through your browser and Calendly’s controls.
